About Ready4Followers

We build intelligent automation tools for social media creators and affiliate marketers.

Who We Are

Ready4Followers is an independent software development organization specializing in social media automation, affiliate marketing tools, and AI-powered content generation. Our tools are designed for creators who want to grow their presence on platforms like Pinterest, Instagram, and TikTok without spending hours on repetitive tasks.

What We Build

Our flagship product is an automated Pinterest affiliate marketing system that discovers trending products, generates AI-optimized pin descriptions, and schedules pins to targeted boards — all on autopilot. We integrate with platforms including AliExpress Affiliate, Pinterest API v5, and leading AI providers.

All tools are built and operated for personal and organizational use, serving our own accounts and content channels.

Our Mission

We believe creators should spend their time creating, not copying and pasting. Our mission is to eliminate the manual, repetitive work of social media management so creators can focus on what matters — building an audience and generating income.
