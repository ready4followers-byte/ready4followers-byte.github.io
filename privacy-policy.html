Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

Short version: Ready4Followers is a personal automation tool. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data from other users. All operations are performed on our own accounts only.

1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Ready4Followers ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with our automation tools and applications. Our tools are built and used exclusively for our own organizational and personal accounts.

2. What Data We Access

Our Pinterest integration accesses the following data from our own Pinterest account only:

  • Pinterest board information (board IDs, board names) — to determine where to post pins
  • Pin creation — to publish affiliate product pins to our own boards
  • Account profile data — to verify authentication

We do not access data from any Pinterest user other than our own account.

3. How We Use Data

Data accessed through the Pinterest API is used solely to:

  • Create and schedule pins on our own Pinterest boards
  • Verify that our automation is functioning correctly

We do not use any accessed data for analytics, advertising, or any purpose beyond direct automation of our own account.

4. Data Storage

We do not store Pinterest user data on any external server or database. Authentication tokens are stored securely in our private self-hosted automation system (n8n) and are never shared with third parties.

5. Third-Party Services

Our automation integrates with the following third-party services:

  • AliExpress Affiliate API — to retrieve product data and generate affiliate links
  • NVIDIA AI / Groq AI — to generate pin descriptions (no Pinterest data is sent to AI providers)
  • Pinterest API v5 — to create pins on our own account

6. No Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share any data with third parties. Our tools operate exclusively on our own accounts and do not process data belonging to other users.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect access credentials and API tokens. All tokens are stored in a private, access-controlled environment and rotated regularly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
